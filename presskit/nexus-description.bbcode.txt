[size=4][i]One look speed in every camera state.[/i][/size]

Sprinting, drawing a bow and casting no longer slow your camera down.[/center]

[line]

[center][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/concise-mouse-consistency-cmc/main/presskit/nexus/section-01-features.png[/img][/center]

Skyrim quietly changes how fast your camera turns. Sprint, draw a bow, or start casting, and horizontal look speed drops to half. Eagle Eye slows it further with game time. Your mouse moves the same distance, but the camera turns less. [b]Concise Mouse Consistency (CMC)[/b] undoes that, and leaves everything else the way you set it.

[list]
[*][b]Full-rate sprint.[/b] Horizontal look stays at freelook speed while sprinting, in first and third person.
[*][b]Steady bow aim.[/b] In first person, horizontal bow and crossbow aim is rebuilt from raw mouse movement, and pitch is normalized to your freelook feel.
[*][b]Casting and other half-rate states.[/b] First-person states that Skyrim halves are restored to full speed.
[*][b]Real-time slow-mo.[/b] During Eagle Eye and other slow-time effects, horizontal look runs at real-time speed in first and third person.
[*][b]Your sensitivity, your way.[/b] One global scale, separate mouse and gamepad X/Y multipliers, bow aim multipliers, and opt-in per-state overlays (walking, running, sprinting, bow aim, magic, one-hand, two-handed, dual wielding). Every overlay ships disabled.
[*][b]Optional third-person smoothing removal,[/b] on by default.
[*][b]Live settings.[/b] Changes in the in-game menu or the INI apply without restarting Skyrim.
[*][b]No plugin.[/b] Just a DLL and an INI, so it takes no load order slot.
[/list]

[center][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/concise-mouse-consistency-cmc/main/presskit/nexus/section-02-how-it-works.png[/img][/center]

[b]What Skyrim does[/b]
[list]
[*]While you sprint, aim a bow in first person or cast in first person, the game scales horizontal turning by 0.5.
[*]Eagle Eye and other slow-time effects slow camera turning along with game time.
[/list]

[b]What CMC changes[/b]
[list]
[*]Each frame, CMC checks the turn scale the game applied. When it is half speed (0.48–0.52×), CMC restores the full turn. Frames already at full speed are left alone.
[*]First-person bow aim rebuilds horizontal look from raw mouse movement. Vertical look keeps the game's own movement, with pitch normalized to freelook.
[*]During slow time, horizontal look is converted back to real-time speed.
[/list]

[b]What it leaves alone[/b]
[list]
[*]Normal freelook. With default settings it turns exactly as before.
[*]Third-person bow aim and casting, which keep Skyrim's or your camera mod's own speed.
[*]FOV and zoom. CMC never scales look speed by either.
[/list]

[center][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/concise-mouse-consistency-cmc/main/presskit/nexus/section-03-requirements.png[/img][/center]

[list]
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/30379]SKSE[/url] for your game version. Skyrim 1.7.99 and 1.7.104 need SKSE 2.3.1.
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/32444]Address Library for SKSE Plugins[/url]. Skyrim 1.7.99 and later need the format-5 [font=Courier New]versionlib-1-7-*.bin[/font] files.
[*][i]Optional:[/i] [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/120352]SKSE Menu Framework[/url] for the in-game settings page. Without it, everything is in an INI.
[/list]
Not required: SkyUI, an ESP, or any patch.

[center][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/concise-mouse-consistency-cmc/main/presskit/nexus/section-04-installation.png[/img][/center]

[b]New install:[/b] install with your mod manager and launch through SKSE. The archive holds two files, [font=Courier New]Data/SKSE/Plugins/MouseSensitivityFix.dll[/font] and [font=Courier New]MouseSensitivityFix.ini[/font]. There's nothing to enable in your load order.

[b]Updating:[/b] just overwrite. If you edited your INI, keep your copy when the mod manager asks. It's safe mid-playthrough.

[b]Uninstalling:[/b] remove the mod. CMC stores nothing in your save.

[center][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/concise-mouse-consistency-cmc/main/presskit/nexus/section-05-settings.png[/img][/center]

Open [b]SKSE Menu Framework[/b] and go to [b]Concise Mouse Consistency / Settings[/b]. Changes apply live. Press [b]Save to INI[/b] to keep them.
[list]
[*][b]Settings:[/b]
[list]
[*]Enabled
[*]Global sensitivity
[*]Mouse X/Y multiplier and gamepad X/Y multiplier
[*]Mouse bow X/Y (first person)
[*]Remove third-person smoothing
[*]Suppress focus spike (alt-tab)
[*]Apply to gamepad look
[*]Keep CMC 3rd-person smoothing removal with camera mods
[*]Save to INI and Reload from INI
[/list]
[*][b]State Overrides:[/b] one page each for Walking, Running, Sprinting, Bow pullback aiming, Magic use, One Hand, Two Handed and Dual Wielding. Each has X/Y sensitivity and first/third-person toggles, and stays disabled until you turn it on.
[/list]
No Menu Framework? Every option is in [i]Data/SKSE/Plugins/MouseSensitivityFix.ini[/i], with comments. Gamepad bow multipliers and other advanced options are only in the INI's [font=Courier New][Advanced][/font] section.

[center][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/concise-mouse-consistency-cmc/main/presskit/nexus/section-06-compatibility.png[/img][/center]

[list]
[*][b]Game versions:[/b] one DLL for SE 1.5.97, AE 1.6.x, AE 1.7.99 / 1.7.104 and GOG. In-game testing so far has been on Steam AE 1.6.1170.
[*][b]SmoothCam and Improved Camera:[/b] compatible. CMC detects them. By default it still removes third-person smoothing; turn off [i]Keep CMC 3rd-person smoothing removal with camera mods[/i] to leave smoothing to the camera mod.
[*][b]Other mouse or sensitivity mods:[/b] anything else that edits look input can stack with CMC. Use one or the other.
[*][b]Skyrim VR:[/b] not supported. From 1.0.2, CMC refuses to load on VR.
[/list]

[center][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/concise-mouse-consistency-cmc/main/presskit/nexus/section-07-faq.png[/img][/center]

[list]
[*][b]Will this change how my normal look feels?[/b] No. With default settings, freelook is untouched. CMC only steps in where Skyrim slows turning.
[*][b]Third-person bow aim still feels slower.[/b] By design for now. Third-person bow aim and casting haven't measured as a clean half-speed state, so CMC leaves them to Skyrim or your camera mod.
[*][b]My first mouse movement after a pause seems to get dropped.[/b] That's [i]Suppress focus spike (alt-tab)[/i]. It ignores the first movement after 350 ms of stillness. Raise [font=Courier New]iFocusSpikeGapMs[/font] or set [font=Courier New]bSuppressFocusSpike=false[/font].
[*][b]A combat mod slows my first-person turning on purpose, and CMC undoes it.[/b] CMC restores any first-person turning that runs at about half speed. Setting [font=Courier New]bEnableFirstPersonHook=false[/font] keeps the other mod's behavior, but turns off all of CMC's first-person corrections.
[*][b]Does it scale with FOV or zoom?[/b] No, on purpose.
[/list]
