[size=4][color=#f2eeea]One look speed in every camera state.[/color][/size]
[color=#89bedd]SKSE plugin · DLL only · No ESP[/color][/center]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/concise-mouse-consistency-cmc/main/presskit/nexus/section-01-about.png[/img]

Skyrim quietly changes how fast your camera turns. Sprint, draw a bow, or start casting, and horizontal look speed drops to half. Eagle Eye slows it further with game time. Your mouse moves the same distance, but the camera turns less.

[b]Concise Mouse Consistency (CMC)[/b] undoes that. Horizontal look stays at freelook speed in each of those states. Everything else stays the way you set it.

[center][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/concise-mouse-consistency-cmc/main/presskit/nexus/cmc-gallery-look-speed-1920x1080.png[/img][/center]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/concise-mouse-consistency-cmc/main/presskit/nexus/section-02-features.png[/img]

[list]
[*][b]Full-rate sprint look[/b]: horizontal look is restored while sprinting, in first and third person.
[*][b]Steady bow aim[/b]: in first person, horizontal bow and crossbow aim is rebuilt from raw mouse movement. Pitch is normalized to your freelook feel.
[*][b]Casting and other half-rate states[/b]: first-person states measured at 0.5× are restored to full rate.
[*][b]Real-time slow-mo[/b]: during Eagle Eye and other slow-time effects, horizontal look runs at real-time speed in first and third person.
[*][b]Sensitivity controls[/b]: one global scale, separate mouse and gamepad X/Y multipliers, and bow aim multipliers.
[*][b]Per-state overlays (opt-in)[/b]: walking, running, sprinting, bow aim, magic, one-hand, two-handed and dual wielding. Every overlay ships disabled.
[*][b]Third-person smoothing removal[/b]: optional, on by default.
[*][b]Alt-tab spike guard[/b]: drops the first mouse movement after a pause of 350 ms or more, where the alt-tab jump lands. Adjustable with [code]iFocusSpikeGapMs[/code], or turn it off with [code]bSuppressFocusSpike=false[/code].
[*][b]Live settings[/b]: changes in the in-game menu or the INI apply without restarting Skyrim.
[/list]

[/list]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/concise-mouse-consistency-cmc/main/presskit/nexus/section-04-installation.png[/img]

[list=1]
[*]Install the main file with Mod Organizer 2, Vortex, or any mod manager.
[*]Launch through SKSE.
[/list]

The archive contains two files: [code]Data/SKSE/Plugins/MouseSensitivityFix.dll[/code] and [code]MouseSensitivityFix.ini[/code]. There is no ESP, ESL or ESM, so it takes no load order slot. You can add or remove it mid-playthrough.

[b]Updating:[/b] overwrite the old files. If you edited your INI, keep your copy when the mod manager asks.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/concise-mouse-consistency-cmc/main/presskit/nexus/section-05-configuration.png[/img]

[list]
[*][b]In game:[/b] SKSE Menu Framework → [i]Concise Mouse Consistency / Settings[/i]. Changes apply right away. Press [b]Save to INI[/b] to keep them.
[*][b]INI:[/b] [code]Data/SKSE/Plugins/MouseSensitivityFix.ini[/code]. Saved edits are picked up while the game is running.
[*]Gamepad bow multipliers and the other advanced settings are in the INI [code][Advanced][/code] section.
[*]Leave [code]bVerboseLogging=false[/code] unless you are collecting logs for a bug report.
[/list]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/concise-mouse-consistency-cmc/main/presskit/nexus/section-06-compatibility.png[/img]

[list]
[*][b]Game versions:[/b] one DLL for SE 1.5.97, AE 1.6.x, AE 1.7.99 / 1.7.104 and GOG. In-game testing so far has been on Steam AE 1.6.1170. [b]Skyrim VR is not supported.[/b] From 1.0.2, CMC refuses to load on VR.
[*][b]SmoothCam / Improved Camera:[/b] compatible. CMC detects them. By default it still removes third-person smoothing; turn [i]Keep third-person smoothing removal with camera mods[/i] off to leave smoothing to the camera mod.
[*][b]Other mouse or sensitivity mods:[/b] anything else that edits look input can stack with CMC. Use one or the other.
[/list]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kexodus/concise-mouse-consistency-cmc/main/presskit/nexus/section-07-known-limits.png[/img]

[list]
[*]Third-person bow aim and third-person casting keep Skyrim's own look speed or your camera mod's. Neither has been measured as a clean half-rate state, so CMC leaves them alone.
[*]CMC does not scale look speed by FOV or zoom.
[*]CMC does not change third-person vertical look (pitch).
[*]First-person horizontal look measured at about half speed (0.48–0.52×) is restored to full speed. A combat or animation mod that deliberately halves turn speed in first person will have that effect undone.
[/list]
